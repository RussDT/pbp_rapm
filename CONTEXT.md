# RAPM Domain Glossary

## Parent RAPM

The total offensive and defensive impact estimate before that impact is divided
into explanatory components.

## Public DECOMP

The reader-facing additive explanation of RAPM. Net-Continuation DECOMP is the
selected forward public product; the gross eight-component surface remains the
compatibility benchmark during migration.

## Gross DECOMP

The former public eight-component actual-points decomposition. It remains a
valid comparison and compatibility surface, but it is not the forward product.

## Net-Continuation DECOMP

The selected forward decomposition. It values a missed first chance together
with its expected continuation and separately accounts for second-chance
conversion. Its public migration status is distinct from any predictive VPM
claim.

## Net DECOMP Actual

The forward explanatory Net DECOMP variant that uses realized continuation-
priced possession outcomes without replacing shot results with expected value.

## Net DECOMP SQ + Residual

The forward explanatory companion that separates Russell ShotQuality v2
expected value from realized-minus-expected shot residuals while retaining the
same net-continuation accounting and common parent alphas.

## TD550 Net SQ VPM

The research-only predictive surface that uses the SQ-plus-residual net targets,
frozen component-specific penalties, and a 550-day half-life. It is not the
uniform-alpha Net DECOMP explanation.

## RAPM Table Registry

The curated definition and generated artifact inventory for impact,
decomposition, predictive, mechanism, role, compatibility, and legacy RAPM
tables. It is the intended contract boundary for a future API.

## VPM / Impact Monster

The predictive player-value model built from stabilized component histories and
priors. VPM predicts future impact; DECOMP explains a fitted RAPM result. The two
must not be used interchangeably.

## Mechanism RAPM

A RAPM whose target is a basketball rate, tendency, role, or event mechanism,
such as rim frequency, three-point percentage, or assisted points. A mechanism
RAPM is a diagnostic surface and is not automatically additive with parent
impact.

## Model Specification

The choices used to estimate a metric, including sample window, regular-season
or playoff scope, rubberband adjustment, time decay, priors, and penalties. A
specification is not a separate metric family.

## Model Status

The methodological trust classification of a model: current, research,
diagnostic, parked/rejected, or recovery.

## Artifact Readiness

Whether the expected files for a declared release window are complete, partial,
missing, or of unknown freshness. Artifact readiness is independent of model
status.

## Legacy / Audit Surface

A superseded metric retained for reproduction, migration checks, or historical
comparison. Legacy does not mean corrupt; it means the surface is not the
incumbent offering.

## Stale Artifact

An output whose methodology may remain valid but whose season window, inputs, or
consumer contract is older than the declared release target.
