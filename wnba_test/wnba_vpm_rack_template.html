About

VPM is databallr's WNBA player impact in points per 100 possessions: a single-season prior-centred ridge over 2026 lineups, where each factor's prior is a public box-score model trained only on 2022–2025. Offense + defense = VPM; defense is positive-good.

Offense and defense each split exactly into four factors — eFG (rim + mid + three shot value), FT, TOV (turnover value, positive = fewer / cheaper turnovers), and 2nd chance. The eFG zones module is the rim / mid / three drill-down of the eFG bar.

This is the box-only lane. No tracking-data priors are used, so missing tracking games do not affect it.

Player profile — databallr's profile modules — career log, shot profile, role rates — re-set in the rack and wired to the board selection above. Every number is this repo's own WNBA box cache or the published VPM history; nothing is sample data.
