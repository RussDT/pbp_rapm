# Chronological shooting fusion

Protocol fixed September 14, 2026 before results. Research-only 3P% harness. Retain current board. 2019 is warm-up/base selection, 2020 trains first fusion, 2021–2022 selects fusion, 2023–2025 is already-spent historical stress. No 2026 outcomes or 2027 data enter. Historical source extraction is retrospective even when model training is chronological.

Inputs at each played game: pregame Kalman 3P% and variance; exponentially discounted 3P% at 30/180/730 days with 20 initial attempts; 180-day discounted FT% with 20 initial attempts; 180-day 3PA/offensive-possession ratio with 200 initial possessions; prior cumulative 3PA/FTA, prior appearances and team-change flag. Every state is read before updating current observations. Initial constants .35 3P%, .75 FT%, .06 attempts/possession are fixed conventions. The Kalman grid q_day={1e-7,1e-6,1e-5}, r={.25,1}, v0=.01, q_game=q_offseason=0 is selected on 2019 only. Attempts weight percentage evidence; zero-attempt games update volume and time. All quantities are finite and missing/invalid box data fail.

Compare Kalman and three decay controls; volume-informed and volume+FT moving priors (prior-season quadratic ridge alpha10, fixed half-strength, Kalman residual); standardized linear residual fusion ridge alpha10/1000; shallow histogram boosting residual correction (100 iterations, learning rate .05, max leaves4, min leaf200, L2=100). No hyperparameter search beyond this fixed list. Base predictions used in second-stage training are pregame. Learned priors and fusion models for season S train only through S−1. Fusion inputs include base/decay discrepancies, FT%, volume, variance and history support. Do not reuse the broadly tuned v1 parameters in these candidates. The old volume-informed model is a separate historical-reference comparator, not an as-of baseline.

Primary: binomial negative log likelihood per attempt, summed by season then equal season weight. Secondary: attempt-weighted squared percentage error. Joint gate: next-game 3PM/100 squared error, weighted by offensive possessions, including zero-attempt games; every model uses the same pregame volume forecast. Game-cluster paired bootstrap. Choose simplest model within one paired SE of development best; fusion must beat the best simpler control by one SE on primary and not lose more than one SE on joint error. No stress-driven changes. Report low volume <1/<3, established volume>=5, first20 appearances, team changes, calibration and by-season outcomes. Multiple slices are diagnostic, not separate selection opportunities. Low-volume conditional accuracy does not identify hypothetical shooting ability under a different shot diet.
