# Decomp RAPM Video Design

## Mood

Elegant, technical, and editorial. This should feel like a premium analytics title sequence rather than a dashboard walkthrough.

## Palette

- Background: `#10110f`
- Panel: `#181a17`
- Ink: `#f3ead7`
- Muted ink: `#b9ad98`
- Hairline: `#3d3b32`
- Offense accent: `#d88948`
- Defense accent: `#5fa9a4`
- Net accent: `#d8be6f`
- Risk accent: `#a94f55`
- Deep accent: `#222a26`

## Typography

- Display: `Fraunces`
- Data and labels: `IBM Plex Mono`

## Motion

- Equations should assemble like measured notation, not bounce.
- Component groups should cascade in with small directional differences.
- Transitions should use warm editorial wipes and blur-through overlays.
- Keep text large and sparse enough to read without pausing.

## Avoid

- Do not say `Alt3` or `weighted factor` on screen.
- Do not use neon sports graphics, box-score tables, or dense CSV screenshots.
- Do not imply the components are direct box-score skills. Keep the lineup-adjusted caveat visible.
