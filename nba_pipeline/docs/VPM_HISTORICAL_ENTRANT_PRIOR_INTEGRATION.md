# Historical entrant priors for the displayed VPM series

Status: full-history scope confirmed by the owner, November 13, 2026. Dated exposure, 18 early entrant model releases, all 30 annual boards and the separate 6,108-date entrant replay are complete. The full-census independent numerical audit passed. The research reader is available; the full-feature base audit and public-reader admission remain pending.

## Objective

The own-only historical VPM boards and the current production preview have different prior regimes. A coherent production-recipe history requires the entrant/background prior inside the Parent lineup solve at each dated cut; adding a rookie value in the reader after the solve would change neither teammates' ratings nor the centered decomposition correctly. Preserve the old own-only files as a separate archive.

## Available inputs and boundaries

- The completed v73/v74 annual/daily source-adapted history is independently audited and explicitly own-only. It is not the full-feature production architecture.
- The full-feature `compact_history_fast_v1` has 6,108 stored dates, 9,416,143 rows and 30 annual native checkpoints through October 1, 2026, but its producer manifest remains `produced_full_daily_full_bank_history_pending_independent_audit`. Treat it as a research input until the separate scientific graph and full-history audit closes. Do not edit its files or markers.
- The production entrant operator is `vpm.harness.current_entrant_prior.apply_entrant_background`; its frozen 11-head background models have release cuts 2018-10-01, 2019-10-01, 2020-12-01 and 2021–25 October 1. There is no earlier saved entrant model. For earlier history, a separately declared backcast or newly fitted past-only early model is required; silent own-only substitution would mix definitions.
- The saved college source begins in 2008, G League game rows in 2019, and draft identity reaches earlier eras. Unknown background and left-censored career exposure must stay explicit. An early model cannot invent college/G League observations or unobserved pre-1997 career possessions.
- The 2014–26 NBA career ledger has dated player-game counts. The pre-2014 `nba_history.parquet` has completed-season aggregates and cannot supply within-season entrant weights by itself. A source-faithful daily replay needs an independently checked pre-2014 player-game exposure ledger from the original ten-player native rows.
- The current preview's 2026 college snapshot was fetched later than the October 1 retrospective cut. It contains completed-season data and no 2026–27 NBA games, but original provider publication vintages remain uncertified.

## Bounded actual pilot

At the 2018-10-01 full-feature annual cut, a read-only solve used the frozen 2018 entrant model, the saved own-only board and native checkpoint, and the production `apply_entrant_background` operator. The source identity alias was taken from the production canonical-name copy. It handled 2,019 player rows, with 657 nonzero experience weights; maximum absolute Total VPM movement was 3.935524. The Parent normal-equation error was 3.96e-15 and native Net closure was 1.90e-15. This establishes numerical compatibility at one annual cut; it is not a daily-history audit or a publication gate.

The separate October 1, 2026 retrospective production-recipe reconstruction and HIB score are recorded in [HOOP_IMPACT_BENCH_RESEARCH.md](HOOP_IMPACT_BENCH_RESEARCH.md). It is one cut, not a completed rookie-aware historical series.
