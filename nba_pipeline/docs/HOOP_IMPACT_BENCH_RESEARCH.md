# Hoop Impact Bench: historical protocol and VPM fit

Research and local scoring snapshot: November 13, 2026. External source: [`lumahoops/hoop-impact-bench` at `e7884ec`](https://github.com/lumahoops/hoop-impact-bench/tree/e7884ec72e18f7423809efe043258db54cac0c48).

## What it tests

Hoop Impact Bench (HIB) grades **one preseason player rating for each predicted season**, in NBA.com `nba_id` and points per 100 possessions above league average. A CSV has exactly `season,nba_id,rating`; for example the `2024-25` row must use a rating available before that season's opening night. A post-opening-night rating would have seen part of the target season. The standard historical window is the six regular seasons **2020-21 through 2025-26**, 7,230 games and 180 team-seasons. [README, “Your file” and “Results”](https://github.com/lumahoops/hoop-impact-bench/blob/e7884ec72e18f7423809efe043258db54cac0c48/README.md)

For each game and team-season, HIB takes **five times the actual-minutes-weighted player rating**. The default Full forecast uses a metric's own rating for every rated player, including rookies and low-minute players; missing ratings become `-2.0`. The companion Established players view also assigns `-2.0` to everyone under 250 minutes in the prior season. These are HIB's fixed scoring rules, so any alternative fallback or minutes assumption produces a custom run. [README, “The rules”](https://github.com/lumahoops/hoop-impact-bench/blob/e7884ec72e18f7423809efe043258db54cac0c48/README.md); [scoring implementation](https://github.com/lumahoops/hoop-impact-bench/blob/e7884ec72e18f7423809efe043258db54cac0c48/eval.py)

The ranking metric is **team-season net rating RMSE**, in points per 100, after shifting each metric's mean team rating to zero **within each season**. It retains scale errors; HIB also reports the uncentered RMSE and offset. Other separate tests are game-margin R² (a fitted straight line with home edge), correlation of team rating with win percentage, and the same tests on the lowest-continuity third of games/teams. It resamples team-seasons 2,000 times to form 90% intervals, rank ranges and paired differences. [README, “The tests”](https://github.com/lumahoops/hoop-impact-bench/blob/e7884ec72e18f7423809efe043258db54cac0c48/README.md); [scoring implementation](https://github.com/lumahoops/hoop-impact-bench/blob/e7884ec72e18f7423809efe043258db54cac0c48/eval.py)

The historical Full forecast published table has centered net errors of **2.79 for xPM, 2.81 for GRBNU, 2.88 for Danny Harlan's EPM projection, 2.95 for Pulse, 3.01 for OpenRAPM, and 3.32 for EPM**. These are the published table's scores under HIB's exact rules, not VPM results or a ranking transferable to another evaluation. The repo warns that historical files were not collected before every scored season and their model designs have different retrospective exposure. [README, “Full forecast” and “The metrics”](https://github.com/lumahoops/hoop-impact-bench/blob/e7884ec72e18f7423809efe043258db54cac0c48/README.md)

It could supply six pre-opening-night *research* cuts if exact cut-to-season mapping, NBA IDs, rating units and coverage are verified. The [daily history](VPM_DAILY_1997_HISTORY.md) also provides dated states, but a HIB historical submission needs one frozen pre-opening-night state per target season; daily within-season states would answer a different question. Neither history has a HIB score in its completion record. [Annual VPM history](VPM_1997_HISTORY.md); [Daily VPM history](VPM_DAILY_1997_HISTORY.md)

The owner-selected v61 is documented as the current default and has a current board, but the historical VPM series was explicitly kept separate. The [full-feature rebuild](VPM_FULL_FEATURE_HISTORY_REBUILD.md) has one audited current mapper/board and all 27 history release maps admitted; the annual/native adapters, measured storage budget and replacement daily history remain unfinished. [VPM model map](VPM_MODEL_MAP.md); [full-feature history rebuild](VPM_FULL_FEATURE_HISTORY_REBUILD.md)

Our game RMSE, team-net and team-win comparisons are not numerically interchangeable with HIB's score: HIB uses its six-season sample, actual game minutes, `-2.0` missing-player rule, its own team target and season centering. The existing [VPM field benchmark](VPM_FIELD_BENCHMARK.md) is relevant background but does not substitute for a HIB harness run. HIB's historical table itself is a retrospective baseline, with variable model-selection exposure; the dated VPM rows protect input chronology but do not undo any later architecture or hyperparameter selection. [HIB README](https://github.com/lumahoops/hoop-impact-bench/blob/e7884ec72e18f7423809efe043258db54cac0c48/README.md); [VPM annual history](VPM_1997_HISTORY.md)

### Historical window: 2020–21 through 2025–26

I exported Total VPM from six annual v73 cuts (November 1, 2020 and October 1, 2021–25), mapping each cut to the *following* season, and from six v74 daily opening-day cuts. The daily producer excludes all games on the query date before emission; the actual cut dates are December 22, 2020; October 19, 2021; October 18, 2022; October 24, 2023; October 22, 2024; and October 21, 2025. The historical six-season HIB v2.0.0 run uses 7,230 games, 180 team-seasons, actual minutes, default `-2.0` missing value and 2,000 team-season resamples. The old annual/daily histories are separate from selected v61 and the new full-feature board. Their retrospective construction and historical source-vintage limits remain.

| HIB view and historical VPM | Centered team net RMSE | Game margin R² | Team win % r |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Full, annual v73 | 3.023 | .173 | .792 |
| Full, daily v74 | 3.024 | .173 | .792 |
| Established, annual v73 | 3.008 | .171 | .791 |
| Established, daily v74 | 3.007 | .171 | .791 |

Full-view v74's centered RMSE interval is 2.764–3.273 (90%); raw RMSE is 3.160 and rated coverage is 90.47% of HIB's established-player minutes. The published full-view table reports OpenRAPM 3.01, LOKI 3.16, EPM 3.32 and leaders xPM 2.79, GRBNU 2.81 and Danny Harlan's EPM projection 2.88. Our paired v74 minus OpenRAPM difference on the locally bundled rating files is +0.018, 90% interval −0.165 to +0.191, so this result does not separate those two. Private third-party rating files were unavailable for paired comparisons with the published top three. The v74 and v73 values are nearly identical under this test; v73 minus v74 is −0.0011, 90% interval −0.0126 to +0.0096.

### Current season: 2026–27 preseason forecasts, through November 12

The local run applies HIB's pinned live v1.1.0 harness and its November 13 checkout of `live/data` to the owner-selected **v61 September 1, 2026** board and the separate audited **full-feature October 1, 2026** research board. Both precede the October 20 opening night. These are fixed preseason ratings, not a dynamic daily entry. The live harness scores 174 games and 30 teams; HIB's companion `centered.py` computes its ranking test from the harness's team ratings and resamples. Both VPM CSVs rate 93% of minutes in the full view; unrated players receive HIB's `-2.0`. The full-feature current board is own-only and distinct from selected v61's rich entrant priors.

**Production distinction (checked November 13):** `nbarapm.com/datasets/VPMPreview` reads `public.nba_vpm_preview_boards`, whose first saved production preview has `snapshot_date=2026-11-02` and whose latest row is dated November 12, covering games through November 11 (170 games, 641 displayed players). The latest feed applies the October 1 full-feature mapper to current data and restores the frozen v61 entrant/background prior before the final solve. There is no production preseason snapshot. Neither frozen HIB result in the table below is an evaluation of that exact production feed. Scoring its November 12 ratings on all games through November 11 would use postgame information; a production dynamic score needs each board saved before the games it predicts. See [nightly runner](VPM_DAILY_RUNNER.md) and the live [VPM Preview](https://nbarapm.com/datasets/VPMPreview).

**Retrospective production-recipe pregame reconstruction:** In response to the owner's follow-up, I re-solved the saved October 1 full-feature board with the frozen production `prepare_background` and `apply_entrant_background` operators, at the same October 1 cutoff. The reconstruction reads the existing October 1 native checkpoint and board, with last native/mapper training game June 13, 2026, and a saved completed-2026 college snapshot. The entrant career preparer reports **0 current-season native games/rows** at this cut; the original board metadata records `protected_2027_inputs=0`. No mapper, entrant model, SQ model or penalty was fitted or changed. The restored board covers 2,856 historical-universe players and 582 with positive prior-year exposure; 1,030 have nonzero experience-blend weights. Its parent normal-equation error is 4.22e-15 and native Net closure 2.04e-15; the zero-background-weight operator reproduces the own-only October 1 board within 6.41e-12. The local artifact is [`prod_preseason_reconstruction/ratings.parquet`](../results/hoop_impact_bench_v1/prod_preseason_reconstruction/ratings.parquet), with a compact [ranking CSV](../results/hoop_impact_bench_v1/prod_preseason_reconstruction/rankings.csv) and [HIB input CSV](../results/hoop_impact_bench_v1/prod_preseason_reconstruction/vpm_prod_recipe_oct1_reconstructed.csv).

Under HIB's frozen Full forecast on 174 games through November 12, that reconstructed production recipe scores **5.736 centered team net RMSE** (90% interval 4.496–6.822), **.272 game-margin R²**, **.829 team win % r**, and **124–50** higher-rated-team picks. The own-only October 1 ancestor scores 5.654, .283, .834 and 127–47 on the identical games. The paired reconstructed-minus-own-only centered RMSE difference is +.082, 90% interval −.033 to +.205, unresolved. These values use HIB's pinned v1.1.0 harness and `centered.py`; the local run is in [`prod_preseason_reconstruction/hib_full`](../results/hoop_impact_bench_v1/prod_preseason_reconstruction/hib_full/). This reconstruction was **computed after the opener**, so it is a causal pregame-cut diagnostic, not a contemporaneously published HIB live entry. The saved college source is a later snapshot of completed-2026 data, and historical source vintages are not certified. The October 1 board excludes players with no earlier admitted NBA history, including new 2026–27 rookies; HIB assigns unrated players `-2.0`.

## Historical entry requirements

1. Export a separate, immutable `season,nba_id,rating` CSV from the **six annual VPM cuts strictly before the relevant opening nights**. Use Total VPM, and verify that its sign and points-per-100 scale match HIB. Preserve the source cut, model vintage and CSV hash.
2. Check NBA ID mapping, duplicates, finite ratings, and the percentage of HIB actual minutes covered. HIB warns when rated coverage of prior-season established players falls below 90% or the established-player rating average is more than 1.5 points from zero. Use HIB's prescribed `-2.0` for missing players in the standard run; do not silently replace our own unknowns with zero. [README, “Your file”](https://github.com/lumahoops/hoop-impact-bench/blob/e7884ec72e18f7423809efe043258db54cac0c48/README.md)
3. Preserve the local v2.0.0 full and Established runs, their `results.json`, per-season and rebuilt-roster rows, and input SHA-256. A shorter window or changed fallback is labelled custom and cannot be compared directly to the standard table. [README, “Quick start,” “Options”](https://github.com/lumahoops/hoop-impact-bench/blob/e7884ec72e18f7423809efe043258db54cac0c48/README.md)
