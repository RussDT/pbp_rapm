Immediate SQ → clock state → second-chance continuation

How our Shot Quality stack sees the whole shot.

We now separate three questions: how good the attempt was, how the clock and shooter changed its make probability, and what the miss was likely to create afterward.

The key distinction: immediate shot EV and possession value at release are not the same object. A shot can look fine as a field-goal attempt and still carry a rebound-geometry cost because the wrong player was pulled away from the rim.

Immediate shot EV

How many points should the field-goal attempt itself score?

P(make | context, shooter, defense, clock) × 2 or 3

Expected continuation

How often will this miss be recovered, and what is that recovery worth?

P(miss) × P(OREB | shooter, zone, lineups) × points / OREB

Release possession EV

The complete pre-result value of choosing this attempt in this configuration.

immediate shot EV + expected continuation EV
Why a “1.10 shot becomes 1.09” needs a baseline: continuation EV is nonnegative in absolute terms. The negative Embiid adjustment is relative to the continuation a neutral shooter/lineup configuration would have generated. Absolute release EV adds continuation; the centered decision-quality scale subtracts the neutral continuation first.

Clock adds real prediction

On 219,159 2026 RS shots, clock conditioning improves Brier by −0.000658 versus the same-fold recalibrated baseline; the game-cluster interval is [−0.000762, −0.000551].

Burden, not automatic credit

Jokić carried 92 recorded grenades and 16.17 expected possession points of burden; Embiid carried 56 and 10.13. The model does not know who caused the late clock or literally handed over the ball.

Current clock limitation: the clock experiment is grouped out of fold on the repo-owned SQ surface, but its historical context maps are not yet a fully expanding daily fit. It is a clean incremental clock result, not yet the final production walk-forward model.
After the miss · the Embiid effect

Who takes the shot changes who can rebound it.

A center taking a long two changes the rebound configuration. The model compares the actual shooter setup with the same-zone shot taken by each teammate while the center moves into the vacated non-shooter role.

Actual configuration

Embiid takes the long two

The other four offensive players form the crash group. The shooter×zone term knows Embiid is the perimeter shooter.

⇄
Role-vacancy counterfactual

A teammate takes the same shot

Embiid moves into that teammate’s non-shooter slot. We score all four swaps and average them equally.

What the target includes

The strict test uses initial-FGA points plus downstream SECOND_CHANCE_CLEAN. First-chance free throws are excluded, so this isolates the shot + rebound branch rather than pretending it is already every point in full PPP.

From shots to impact

How Shot Quality enters RAPM—and where the overlays stop.

The production shot model estimates immediate event value. RAPM then asks which players consistently change the quality of attempts created and allowed while controlling for lineups. Clock-conditioned and continuation-aware value are still research layers.

Possible VPM prior

Stabilized summaries only

VPM should never ingest raw provider defender joins, event rows, or fold predictions. Only reliable multi-season player summaries—such as grenade burden or clock-adjusted shooting talent—may graduate after incremental tests.
