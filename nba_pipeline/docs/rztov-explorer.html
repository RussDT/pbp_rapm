Method & evidence

How rZTOV works

Every scoring attempt and potential assist earns a turnover allowance. rZTOV measures actual turnovers above or below the allowance created by that workload. Turnovers stay inside the play denominator, matching the shape of box-score TOV%.

The formula

Negative rZTOV and positive TOV saved are good. Each season is priced by the joint fit on the five-season window ending that year. Points price each lane separately: a through-origin fit of that window's net-continuation lane RAPM on turnovers saved in the lane.
