## Legacy PBP Notes

- Raw fetched PBP does not populate `player2_*` assist fields, so assist-derived metrics need to parse the `(... N AST)` description tag.
- The assisted-points hierarchy expands each standard possession to five focal-player opportunities and fits a passer-minus-receiver contrast on the common fully resolved sample. Final passer and receiver RAPMs are additive children of offensive `ASSIST_POINTS`, recovered as `(parent ± contrast) / 2`.
- The scoring-role hierarchy defines on-ball as taking the scoring shot/FT; each of the four non-shooters receives one quarter of the off-ball target. The fitted role contrast is anchored to `SCORING_PARENT` with `(parent ± contrast) / 2`. Interpret off-ball as adjusted teammate scoring while not the shooter, not tracked movement or screening.
- Prefer the TS shot-role hierarchy for a turnover-free shooter/nonshooter split. `TS_SELF_SHOT` and `TS_TEAMMATE_SHOT` fit conditional focal effects, anchor their contrast to parent oTS at observed shot share, and use an exact self-shot + teammate-shot + shot-mix Shapley allocation relative to a 20% reference share. Interpret the conditional teammate-shot rate as the direct nonshooter diagnostic, not as optical off-ball tracking.
- Prefer the joint centered shooter-interaction model for final oTS role results. It uses one attempt row, centers the player role indicator by player-season shot share, chooses role shrinkage with grouped game CV plus the one-SE rule, and closes joint parent oTS exactly through direct self-shot and teammate-shot contributions. Retain the separate-role Shapley hierarchy only as an audit prototype.
- Raw fetched PBP can contain duplicated event rows for the same game; normalize `game_id` to a stable 10-character string and dedupe exact rows before writing or processing seasonal raw parquet files.
- Per-FGA shot-clock estimation should sort raw events by `game_id`, `period`, `clock_sec` descending, then `event_num` ascending; corrected NBA PBP events can carry late event numbers at an earlier clock.
- Shot-clock SQ, grenade burden, second-chance opportunity, defender-role, and state/event-value models are RAPM-owned overlays. Score actual and neutral clocks with the same held-out model and preserve the exact EV bridge. VPM receives only stabilized player-season summaries after reliability tests, never raw shot/event/provider rows or fold predictions.
- The PBP-only own-shooter playtype difficulty proxy uses six broad outcome-invariant description buckets, ignores make/miss and made-only assist tags, and prices the current shot mix from the immediately prior regular season. Keep every future-oTS comparison creation-controlled with feature-season on-ball percentage, potential assists/100, and rim assists/100 in every model. Use the decompressed floating-three specification: raw `pts100`, `TSA100`, and `2*TSA100*difficulty/100` enter separately; do not substitute legacy `TS_added_100` when interpreting the role coefficient. Treat 2025-2026 as spent development evidence; require untouched 2026-27 confirmation and then the summed future-possession/VPM gate.
